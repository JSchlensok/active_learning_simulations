  • Most Logan50 hits are not collagenases. of hits align only to ColH's two PKD domains, a module common in bacterial surface proteins, and another add the collagen-binding domain.
  • hits include part of the peptidase unit, and span all six mature domains. The closest full-length relative, , shares identity over of ColH. No hit is ColH itself.
  • The full-length relatives are clostridial collagenases. Their taxonomy against UniRef50 places 42 of 65 in the Bacillota, mostly Clostridium, and their closest UniRef50 entries are Clostridium, Tissierella and Paenibacillus proteins. Many were assembled from genome runs of animals (porpoise, seals, muskox, fish), which carry bacteria from the sample along with the host.
  • The peptidase-unit hits also include Leptospira (25 hits covering the activator and catalytic subdomains), Bacteroidota, Pseudomonadota and Chloroflexota. The PKD-only hits spread across Bacteria and Archaea, so most resolve only above phylum level.
  • In the PDB, 9LRK's closest neighbours are ColH's own crystal structures (4ARF, 4U6T), the collagenases ColG and ColT of Clostridium, and two Vibrio-type collagenases. The other confident hits are proteins whose PKD-like β-sandwich or α-helical repeat domains resemble single ColH domains.
  • The Logan50 list is capped at the 10,000 best prefilter candidates, so weaker homologs beyond those are missing (Methods).
Query

ColH and its domains

ColH is secreted as a 1021-residue precursor. Behind the signal peptide and propeptide lie the collagenase unit (activator domain, zinc-binding catalytic subdomain and helper subdomain), two polycystic-kidney-disease-like domains (PKD1, also called S2a, and PKD2 or S2b) and the collagen-binding domain (S3). This report colours ColH's three functional units the same way throughout.

The structure search used 9LRK, apo wild-type ColH by cryo-EM at 2.2 Å (Nature Communications, 2026). It models residues 45–808; PKD2 and the collagen-binding domain are not resolved.

Sequence search · MMseqs2 · Logan50

Relatives among 3 billion assembled proteins

Logan50 holds the complete genes that Prodigal predicts in the Logan v1.0 assemblies of public SRA runs (up to December 2023), clustered at 50% identity and without runs classified as human-associated. Each hit is a cluster representative, so hit counts measure sequence diversity, not abundance. MMseqs2 searched the full ColH precursor on an H100 GPU and kept alignments with E ≤ 10−3.

Search cap. MMseqs2 aligned the 10,000 best prefilter candidates, of which passed E ≤ 10−3. The intended cap of 100,000 crashed the GPU prefilter on Logan50 twice, so homologs ranked below the first 10,000 candidates are not in this list. They would be weaker, most likely more PKD and collagen-binding domain proteins.

Structure search · Foldseek · PDB

Structural neighbours of 9LRK

Foldseek compared 9LRK with the PDB as of 1 January 2025 (324,204 cluster representatives, so 9LRK itself is not among them). Of hits, have a homology probability of at least 0.9; the chart and table show those.

Methods

How the searches ran

Query
UniProt Q46085 (entry version 143, sequence version 1), full precursor; PDB 9LRK (model revision 1.1), chain A.
Sequence search
MMseqs2 18-8cc5c, easy-search --gpu 1 --max-seqs 10000, other parameters default (E ≤ 10−3), on one H100 via the lab's mmseqs-logan50 wrapper, which copies the 400 GB database to RAM first. Job 42126564, 1 h 08 min, of which 52 min went to reading hit headers from lustre.
The cap of 100,000
Jobs 42125387 and 42126564 (on two different nodes) both died in the GPU prefilter with --max-seqs 100000 (“CUDA error: an illegal memory access / illegal instruction”). The same setting ran fine against PDB seqres and UniRef50 (test job 42126363), and the lab's Logan50 test passed with the default of 300, so the failure is specific to Logan50 with a large cap.
Structure search
Foldseek 10-941cd33, easy-search with defaults (3Di + amino acids) on CPU, which unlike GPU returns taxonomy; PDB snapshot 2025-01-01. Job 42125259, 27 s. Its prefilter filled its default cap of 1000 candidates; the confident hits rank well inside it.
Run metadata
ENA portal API, 29 October 2026: organism, library source and study for each SRA run named in a hit header.
Taxonomy
MMseqs2 easy-taxonomy (approximate 2bLCA, taxonomy defaults otherwise) of the 7,879 hit sequences against UniRef50 2026_03 with its NCBI taxonomy, on CPU with the precomputed index. Job 42130846, 4.7 min. It sets --max-rejected 2147483647: with the default of 5, the second alignment pass stops after five consecutive misses, often before it reaches the top hit, and left 1,630 hits unassigned (job 42130720) although a plain search finds strong UniRef50 matches for them (job 42130807). The closest UniRef50 entry and its identity come from a separate easy-search with full alignments (job 42130957), because the taxonomy workflow aligns in score-only mode and only estimates identities.
Domain assignment
A hit covers a ColH domain when its alignment spans at least half of it. Classes: peptidase unit if it covers the activator, catalytic or helper subdomain; otherwise collagen-binding domain; otherwise PKD domains.

Caveats

  • The Logan50 list holds the 10,000 best prefilter candidates only (see above).
  • Logan50 clusters proteins at 50% identity, and the run named in a hit is the cluster representative's.
  • A run's organism describes the sample, not necessarily the gene: bacterial genes appear in animal and plant genome runs.
  • Lowest-common-ancestor assignments are conservative: a hit whose UniRef50 matches span phyla is assigned above them, even when its single closest match is a named genus.
  • The GPU prefilter of MMseqs2 differs from the CPU one and finds somewhat more hits.
  • 9LRK lacks PKD2 and the collagen-binding domain, so the structure search cannot find relatives of those two.
